# Learning Loop

Learning should improve continuity without turning one edit or result into an invisible permanent rule.

## Inputs

Use only evidence the founder explicitly supplies or approves for the private record:

- final edits and the reasons behind them;
- accepted and rejected recommendations;
- explicit preferences or corrections;
- publication decisions;
- replies or qualitative feedback;
- performance results with sufficient context;
- changes in reader, promise, business, cadence, or boundaries.

Do not treat missing feedback as approval. Do not retrieve or aggregate private audience data without permission.

## Durable versus situational

A lesson is more likely durable when it:

- repeats across multiple decisions;
- is explicitly stated as a standing preference or boundary;
- changes the publication strategy;
- corrects a material misunderstanding;
- explains why a recurring recommendation was accepted or rejected.

A lesson is more likely situational when it:

- responds to one issue's structure or mood;
- depends on a temporary campaign or event;
- reflects performance without a credible mechanism;
- is an edit made for space or timing;
- conflicts with another recent decision and has not been clarified.

When uncertain, leave it in the issue record rather than promoting it.

## Proposal protocol

For each consequential proposed lesson:

1. **Observe:** describe the edit, decision, or result without interpretation inflation.
2. **Propose:** state the durable rule or updated understanding.
3. **Locate:** identify the exact private file and section that would change.
4. **Diff:** show the current text and proposed replacement/addition in human-readable form.
5. **Explain:** say why it may be durable and why it may instead be situational.
6. **Confirm:** ask approve, revise, reject, or leave pending.
7. **Apply:** change only the approved private record and record the date/source.
8. **Reverse:** preserve enough history to undo or supersede the lesson.

Rejected proposals remain visible with a concise reason so the agent does not keep suggesting them.

## Metrics discipline

Results can trigger questions; they do not automatically rewrite editorial strategy.

- Record time window, audience size, denominator, distribution conditions, and comparison.
- Distinguish opens, clicks, replies, conversions, unsubscribes, and qualitative responses.
- Do not optimize one metric at the expense of the confirmed reader promise without discussion.
- Avoid causal claims from one send.

## Shared product learning boundary

Private Editorial Memory never flows into the distribution automatically. A general product improvement requires a separate process using one of:

- explicit feedback;
- a founder-approved and redacted example;
- an anonymized pattern with no re-identification risk;
- an approved case study.

When shared intelligence is updated, installed profiles receive it only after an explicit distribution update. If the new guidance conflicts with private decisions, explain the conflict and propose reassessment rather than overwriting private state.
