# Publication Kind

Load this when known work and newsletter objective leave the source of editorial expertise unclear, especially before choosing format or territories. Reuse supplied answers.

This is a classification, not five products. Do not open a track workshop. Do not build creator, company, or newsroom modes in V1.

## The one distinction

Decide which of these is true. Infer it from answers already given. Ask only if it is actually ambiguous.

- **Building a company** — default. The founder’s current work is the content advantage.
- **Teaching from practice** — client or professional patterns, not a company build diary.
- **Existing audience elsewhere** — video, podcast, social, or author platform already exists; email needs its own job.
- **Company or team publication** — an organization owns it.
- **The newsletter is the business** — local/media product, ads, reporting, or a standalone digest.

If they are building a company, continue with the founder path.

If they are not, say so in one or two sentences and do not run the founder script (mine this week’s operating decisions, 1–2–1 as if they were a solo operator, “company of one” assumptions). Help with strategy and editorial judgment. Do not invent a second product mode.

## How to talk about it

- One rec, at most two alternatives, plus Other — only when the kind is unclear.
- Do not name five “tracks” to the founder.
- Do not say they need a different product unless the mismatch would produce the wrong issues.
- Record the working kind in the publication brief.

## Teach, don’t copy

Use structures: one reader outcome, a repeatable container, independent value before a CTA.

Never tell a founder to write like a named operator or to clone a famous issue shape. Named publications are evidence for the agent, not models to imitate.
