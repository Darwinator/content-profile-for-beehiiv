# Idea Judgment

Selection includes restraint. Recency, business importance, and ease of drafting do not by themselves make an issue worth publishing.

## Is this worth the reader's attention?

Look for a recognizable reader situation, an independent useful payoff, credible proximity, and enough specific evidence or argument to earn attention. Check what this adds beyond previous issues and familiar advice. An accurate source can still be wrong for this founder, this reader, and this promise.

Connect the angle to the founder's current or intended business, objective, expertise, or business and industry interests. Personal interests can enrich voice without becoming content pillars. Check that commercial relationships are transparent and the issue is useful without a purchase.

Judge originality internally and explain the consequence when useful: standard advice may need a narrower reader, stronger example, or a different judgment. A crowded subject with distinctive proof can deserve an issue. Unknown landscape coverage is not proof of sameness. Likewise, uncertain future supply does not veto a worthwhile issue; discuss sustainability when planning cadence or territories, not as an issue eligibility rule.

## Find the right treatment

A full issue needs enough substance for its promise. A section or short note can suit a smaller contribution. A promising claim without evidence may need investigation, a narrower treatment, or time. Reject material that remains misleading, confidential, self-serving, repetitive, or too slight. Explain a material rejection rather than hiding why the recommendation changed.

Use bounded research when it can answer the actual gap. If the central claim is unsupported, develop a defensible alternative or name the exact evidence or permission required. Do not manufacture a quote, result, or authority to make an angle work.

## Recommend, then develop

If direction is already explicit, develop it. Otherwise recommend the strongest opportunity with its reader payoff, specific reason, and any material gap or risk. Return at most three choices, fewer when fewer deserve attention. There is no field quota for each option or compulsory rejection appendix. A tempting “Not now” idea needs only a concise reason.

For a genuinely long comparison, follow `references/delivery-continuity.md`; ordinary recommendations stay inline. Public sources use public, non-sensitive URLs only. Private/local sources use IDs and descriptions.

Keep a compact idea entry and link selected material to the evolving issue record rather than copying the rationale into successive ledgers. Preserve the founder's actual choice and useful parked/rejected reasons so next week's recommendations improve.
